## CI adoption decisions

The [hosted pilot results](v0.8-hosted-pilot-results.md) retain the complete measurements and failed assumptions. Selective cache preparation reduced observed whole-job medians from 320 to 42 seconds, but automatic Cachix writing remains disabled until matched upload and reader costs prove a net benefit. The successful image-verification experiment measured the same 233-second whole-job median cold and warm, so warm pull timing alone justifies no adoption.

Optimized small-job caches and original qualified ISO preservation remain enabled for all four channels. Inventory, Grype DB and whole-qualification reuse remain disabled by default; the DB seed exceeded its transport bound and the security experiment did not complete all lanes. No release gate or cache eligibility check was relaxed.
