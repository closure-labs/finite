## Renamed candidates and the corrected gate

The owner's cancelled [pre-rename producer](https://github.com/closure-labs/finite/actions/runs/38811504667)
was not resumed. The first renamed producer, [38816315997](https://github.com/closure-labs/finite/actions/runs/38816315997),
failed Finite Dev security because a retired `-dx-` name heuristic selected the
wrong foundation. Its remaining qualification was cancelled. [PR #178](https://github.com/closure-labs/finite/pull/178)
introduced explicit profile/foundation mappings with positive and negative
regression cases. Both released candidates came from the corrected new producer;
no failed candidate was released or cleared through an exception.

Finite uses Bluefin and Finite Dev uses Bluefin-DX, preserving upstream kernel,
ZFS and other modules together. Next kernels and variants are retired. See the
[release plan and migration instructions](v0.8.1-release-plan.md) and
[upstream preservation policy](upstream-kernel-variants.md). Passed VM
qualification does not establish physical hardware or ZFS pool workload results.
Passing vulnerability policy does not mean zero advisories.

## Reuse observations and next cycle

Release preparation authenticated native inventory reuse for `finite` and
`finite-dev`. Each reader still updated its vulnerability database, rescanned the
exact candidate and evaluated current policy. These production hits have no
controlled cold comparison, so they establish use rather than a new savings estimate.

| Current candidate | Producer scan step | Release scan step with reused inventory | Observed difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| finite | 9m53s | 2m12s | 7m41s |
| finite-dev | 17m01s | 1m57s | 15m04s |

These are one producer/release pair per exact candidate under independent runner
and event conditions. They are scan-step observations, not a controlled benchmark,
an observed production hit rate or a whole-pipeline savings estimate. Public job
IDs and timestamps are retained in the machine record.

Inventory and whole-qualification reuse remain enabled through repository
overrides, with local/source defaults false. The [new OS measurements](new-os-reuse-results.md)
retain the per-hit savings and their limits. Automatic Cachix writing and Grype
database transport remain disabled. No old OS or retired Next benchmark was run
for this release.

The next development cycle uses `VERSION=0.8.2-dev`. A future stable 0.8.2 release
requires a reviewed numeric version and dated changelog entry before qualified
release preparation. Stable publication continues to reject prerelease versions.
