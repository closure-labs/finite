## Campaign protocol

Before dispatch, record the hypothesis/design change, immutable source/products,
runner/tool/runtime cohorts, cache temperature, sample order, correctness and
performance gates, estimated runner hours and full failed-work accounting.
Each iteration must explain how its redesign answers the prior result. Never
replace selected failed or mismatched samples with faster ones.

Run cheap capability/cohort checks before ISO/VM work. Installer, compression and
new guest-transport comparisons require both profiles, five matched warm pairs
and one cold pair. Each warm complete phase-job and workflow median must improve
by at least 10 percent and 30 seconds. Cold phase/workflow cost may regress no
more than 10 percent. Include producer setup, transfers, hashing, retention and
cleanup; nested stages overlap native job cost. Preserve existing Home Manager
evaluation and release-transport contracts where experiment-specific gates differ.

Missing/stale reusable inputs fall back to fresh work; present corrupt/forged
installer or product evidence fails validation. Every guest/installer change
requires both profiles' fresh and upgrade/rollback acceptance: Secure Boot,
encrypted root, enforcing SELinux, both Nix sockets, actual Home Manager
activation and persistence/customization. Keep inherited initializer settings
and invoke initialization once.

Automatic Cachix writing and Grype DB transport remain disabled. Retained local
OCI verification is deferred until exact same-build export is feasible;
registry-only diagnostics do not prove that alternative. MicroVMs cannot replace
required UEFI/Secure Boot installation coverage. Use recent runs now; preserve
`2026-10-30T05:01:17Z` as a separate complete-window assessment, not a prerequisite.
